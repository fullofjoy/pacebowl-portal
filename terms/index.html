What Are the Terms & Veterinary Limits of PaceBowl?

PaceBowl provides free educational pet nutrition calculators for informational planning only and does not provide veterinary medical diagnosis or treatment.

  • Consult Your Veterinarian: Always consult a licensed veterinarian before putting a pet on a weight-loss diet, transitioning to raw food, or managing medical conditions such as diabetes, pancreatitis, or kidney disease.
  • Verify Bag Labels: Pet food manufacturers periodically reformulate kcal/cup and kcal/kg values; always check the current Guaranteed Analysis panel on your physical bag or can.
  • Free Personal & Clinic Use: Pet owners, shelters, and veterinary clinics may freely use and print PaceBowl A4 feeding charts for non-commercial care.
