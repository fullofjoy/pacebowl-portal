How to Predict Your Puppy's Adult Weight

  1. 1

    Weigh your puppy in lbs or kg

    For small puppies, hold them on a bathroom scale and subtract your own weight.

  2. 2

    Enter exact age in weeks

    Use 13 weeks for 3 months, 17 weeks for 4 months, and 26 weeks for 6 months.

  3. 3

    Select the expected breed size category

    Toy and small breeds finish growing around 38–44 weeks, while large and giant breeds grow until 60–78 weeks.

  4. 4

    Save your milestone growth chart PDF

    Download the A4 PDF to log bi-weekly vet weigh-ins and plan adult bowl and crate sizing.

Exact Formula & Worked Example

growth_fraction = min(1.0, (age_weeks / mature_weeks) ^ 0.72)
est_adult_kg    = current_kg / growth_fraction
range_band      = est_adult_kg * [0.92, 1.08]

Worked Example: A 16-week-old Large Breed puppy (mature age ~64 weeks) weighing 22 lbs (10.0 kg) has completed roughly 37% of adult growth, predicting an adult weight of ~59.7 lbs (range 54.9 – 64.5 lbs / 24.9 – 29.3 kg).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I estimate my puppy's adult weight?

Toy and small breeds reach 50% of adult weight around 14-16 weeks, medium breeds around 16-18 weeks, and large/giant breeds around 20-24 weeks. Our calculator adjusts the growth curve specifically for your breed size category.

When do large breed puppies stop growing?

Large breeds (55-95 lbs, such as Golden Retrievers and Labradors) finish most skeletal growth around 14 to 16 months (60-68 weeks), while giant breeds (95+ lbs) continue filling out until 18 to 24 months.

Should large-breed puppies eat large-breed puppy food?

Yes. Large-breed puppy formulas control calcium-to-phosphorus ratios and calorie density so joints grow steadily without orthopedic stress.
