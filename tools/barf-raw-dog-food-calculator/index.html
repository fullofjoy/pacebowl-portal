How to Calculate Daily BARF or PMR Raw Dog Food Portions

  1. 1

    Enter your dog's ideal weight

    Use ideal target body weight in lbs or kg rather than overweight weight.

  2. 2

    Choose BARF (70/10/10/10) or PMR (80/10/10)

    BARF includes 10% dog-safe vegetables, seeds, and fruit; PMR is 100% animal-based.

  3. 3

    Select the daily body-weight percentage

    Start at 2.5% for healthy adult maintenance, 2.0% for weight loss, 3.0% for active dogs, or 4%–8% for puppies.

  4. 4

    Set your freezer batch days and export the A4 PDF

    Choose 7, 14, or 30 days to generate an exact butcher shopping list in lbs and grams.

Exact Formula & Worked Example

Daily Total (g)     = weight_kg * 1000 * (body_weight_pct / 100)
Component Daily (g) = Daily Total (g) * (component_pct / 100)
Batch Total (lbs)   = (Component Daily (g) * prep_days) / 453.592

Worked Example: A 50 lb (22.68 kg) adult dog fed at 2.5% body weight eats 567 g (20.0 oz) per day. Under the BARF model, that equals 397 g muscle meat (70%), 57 g raw meaty bone (10%), 28 g liver (5%), 28 g secreting organ (5%), 40 g vegetables (7%), 11 g seeds/nuts (2%), and 6 g fruit (1%). Over a 14-day freezer batch, buy 12.25 lbs of muscle meat and 1.75 lbs of raw meaty bone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between BARF and PMR 80/10/10 raw dog food?

BARF (Biologically Appropriate Raw Food) uses 70% muscle meat, 10% raw meaty bone, 5% liver, 5% other secreting organ, 7% vegetables, 2% seeds/nuts, and 1% fruit. PMR (Prey Model Raw) omits plant matter and uses 80% muscle meat, 10% bone, 5% liver, and 5% other secreting organ.

What percentage of body weight should I feed a raw-fed dog?

Most healthy adult dogs thrive at 2.5% of ideal adult body weight per day. Overweight or low-activity dogs typically drop to 2.0%, highly active dogs need 3.0%, and growing puppies require 4% to 8% depending on age.

Can I prep 14 days of raw dog food in advance?

Yes. Use the Batch Prep Days field above (7, 14, or 30 days) to calculate exact total pounds and grams of muscle meat, raw meaty bones, liver, and secreting organs for freezer meal prep.
