Frequently Asked Questions

What is the safest 7-day dog food transition ratio?

Veterinarians recommend Days 1-2: 75% old food + 25% new food; Days 3-4: 50% old + 50% new; Days 5-6: 25% old + 75% new; Day 7: 100% new food. For sensitive stomachs or puppies, extend to a 10-day or 14-day schedule.

Why do calorie densities matter when switching dog food?

If your old kibble has 340 kcal/cup and your new kibble has 440 kcal/cup, feeding the same number of cups increases daily calories by 29%. PaceBowl automatically adjusts new-food grams and cups so total calories stay constant.

What if my dog gets loose stools during a food transition?

Pause at the current ratio for 2 to 3 extra days before increasing the new food percentage, and consider adding 1 tablespoon of plain unsweetened pumpkin puree per meal.
