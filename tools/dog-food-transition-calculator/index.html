How to Switch Dog Food Safely Without Upset Stomach

  1. 1

    Pick a 7-day, 10-day, or 14-day schedule

    Use 7 days for healthy adults, or 10–14 days for puppies, seniors, and sensitive stomachs.

  2. 2

    Enter your current daily old food grams and cups

    Input the total daily amount your dog currently eats across all meals.

  3. 3

    Enter both bags' kcal/cup values

    PaceBowl automatically scales the new food's final grams and cups so total daily calories stay identical.

  4. 4

    Print the fridge checklist and mix thoroughly

    Combine old and new kibble in a slow-feeder bowl and check off each day on your printed A4 sheet.

Exact Formula & Worked Example

Target Daily kcal   = old_daily_cups * old_kcal_per_cup
New Full Daily Cups = Target Daily kcal / new_kcal_per_cup
New Full Daily (g)  = old_daily_grams * (old_kcal_per_cup / new_kcal_per_cup)
Day i Mix           = (old_pct% * Old Full) + (new_pct% * New Full)

Worked Example: If your dog eats 280 g (2.5 cups) of a 360 kcal/cup kibble (900 kcal/day) and you switch to a denser 410 kcal/cup formula, feeding 2.5 cups would overfeed by 14%. PaceBowl adjusts the final Day 7 portion to 246 g (2.20 cups) so daily calories remain locked at 900 kcal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the safest 7-day dog food transition ratio?

Veterinarians recommend Days 1-2: 75% old food + 25% new food; Days 3-4: 50% old + 50% new; Days 5-6: 25% old + 75% new; Day 7: 100% new food. For sensitive stomachs or puppies, extend to a 10-day or 14-day schedule.

Why do calorie densities matter when switching dog food?

If your old kibble has 340 kcal/cup and your new kibble has 440 kcal/cup, feeding the same number of cups increases daily calories by 29%. PaceBowl automatically adjusts new-food grams and cups so total calories stay constant.

What if my dog gets loose stools during a food transition?

Pause at the current ratio for 2 to 3 extra days before increasing the new food percentage, and consider adding 1 tablespoon of plain unsweetened pumpkin puree per meal.
