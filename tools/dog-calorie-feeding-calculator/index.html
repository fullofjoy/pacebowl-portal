Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories does a 50 lb dog need per day?

A healthy 50 lb (22.7 kg) neutered adult dog has a Resting Energy Requirement (RER) of 727 kcal and needs approximately 1,164 kcal/day (1.6x RER), or about 3.23 cups (328 g) of standard 360 kcal/cup kibble split into two meals.

How do I calculate grams of kibble per day from calories?

Check the Guaranteed Analysis panel on your dog food bag for kcal/kg (usually 3,200 to 4,200 kcal/kg). Divide your dog's daily kcal requirement by kcal/kg and multiply by 1,000 to get exact grams per day.

How much should I reduce calories for dog weight loss?

Veterinary weight-loss plans typically target 1.0x RER based on your dog's ideal target weight—not current overweight weight—aiming for safe weight loss of 1% to 2% of body weight per week.
