How Does PaceBowl Calculate Daily Pet Calories?

Every PaceBowl feeding calculator follows the peer-reviewed WSAVA (World Small Animal Veterinary Association) and NRC energy equations:

  • Step 1 — Resting Energy Requirement (RER): RER = 70 × (Body Weight in kg)^0.75. For example, a 20 kg (44 lb) dog has an RER of 662 kcal/day.
  • Step 2 — Daily Energy Requirement (DER): Multiply RER by the pet's life-stage factor (e.g., 1.6× for a neutered adult dog = 1,059 kcal/day; 1.2× for an indoor neutered cat = 262 kcal/day at 10 lbs).
  • Step 3 — Gram & Cup Conversion: Divide DER by your food bag's exact kcal/kg or kcal/cup label (typically 320–420 kcal/cup) and split evenly across 2 or 3 slow-feeder meals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are PaceBowl pet feeding calculators?

PaceBowl uses the official WSAVA and NRC Resting Energy Requirement formula RER = 70 x (weight in kg)^0.75 multiplied by veterinary life-stage factors (1.0x to 3.0x), matching clinical nutrition guidelines.

Why should I weigh pet food in grams instead of scoops?

Veterinary studies show measuring cups can vary by 18% to 48% per scoop, which causes gradual pet obesity. Weighing kibble or raw portions in grams on a digital scale keeps daily calories accurate within 2%.

Are the printable A4 fridge feeding charts free?
