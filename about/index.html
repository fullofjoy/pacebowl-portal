About PaceBowl Pet Nutrition

PaceBowl combines two proven pillars of canine and feline longevity: measuring the exact gram portion your pet needs, and pacing mealtime with the right bowl size.

Why Was PaceBowl Pet Nutrition Created?

Over 55% of household dogs and cats are clinically overweight, largely due to vague pet-food bag ranges and inconsistent scoop measurements. We built PaceBowl so any pet parent can calculate veterinary-standard daily grams in under 15 seconds, print a clean A4 fridge chart for the whole household, and match meal volume to the right slow-feeder bowl—100% free and with zero signup walls.

  • Zero-Login Privacy: Every calculation and A4 PDF chart is generated locally in your browser.
  • Open Math: Every tool publishes a plain-text algorithm.md specification and cites its primary veterinary standard.
  • Continuous Calibration: Spot a formula edge case? Use the floating Feedback button on any page—our editorial team reviews variance reports within 48 hours.
